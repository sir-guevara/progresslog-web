ProgressLog

Privacy Policy

Last updated .

The short version. ProgressLog has no servers and no analytics. Everything you log lives in your personal iCloud private database. We never see it, never collect it, and never share it with anyone.

1. What ProgressLog stores

When you use the app, the following data is created and stored on your device and in your iCloud private database:

  • Your profile (name, email if you provide it, age, sex, height, weight goals, units preference)
  • Your workout templates, scheduled days, logged sets, reps, and weights
  • Your cardio sessions (duration, incline, pace, calories)
  • Your weigh-ins and body measurements (body fat %, waist, chest, arms, thighs)
  • Your meal logs (calories and protein)
  • Progress photos you choose to add
  • Badges you've earned
  • Your preferences (notification times, reminder toggles, app icon choice)
  • The Sign in with Apple identifier (if you sign in) — used only to recognize you on your devices

2. Where it lives

All of the above is stored:

  • Locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework
  • Synced to your personal iCloud private database via Apple's CloudKit framework

This is the same private space Apple uses for things like your Reminders and Notes. It is end-to-end protected by Apple — we have no access to it.

3. What ProgressLog does not do

  • We do not operate any servers
  • We do not collect analytics or telemetry
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone
  • We do not associate your data with a marketing profile
  • We do not see your Health data, workouts, weight, photos, or meals

4. Apple Health

If you grant Apple Health permission, ProgressLog will read your weight, active and basal energy, steps, and workout history from HealthKit, and write your weigh-ins and finished workouts back. This exchange happens locally on your device and never crosses any network we control. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → ProgressLog.

5. In-app purchases

ProgressLog offers an optional $7.99 one-time "Supporter" purchase. The purchase is handled by Apple's StoreKit; we receive only the entitlement status (purchased or not) — never your payment details, name, or address.

6. Notifications

If you enable reminders, ProgressLog schedules local notifications on your device (weigh-in reminders, workout-day reminders). These are entirely local. ProgressLog does not send remote push notifications.

7. Children

ProgressLog is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and every piece of data ProgressLog has stored — on this device and from your iCloud — at any time:

  • Open the app → More → Delete account & all data

This action is immediate and permanent.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever change anything material about how data is handled, we'll update this page and bump the date at the top. There are no servers, so substantive changes are rare.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@progresslog.org and we'll get back to you.
